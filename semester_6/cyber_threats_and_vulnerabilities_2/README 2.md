## Overview
**Divide & Defend** simulates a complete Security Operations Center (SOC) environment integrating threat detection, intelligence correlation, and risk management within a micro-segmented architecture.  
Built on **Wazuh**, **Suricata**, **OpenCTI**, **Grafana**, and **Promtail/Loki**, this lab demonstrates real-time monitoring, automated vulnerability assessment, and intelligence-driven defense.
