**Objective**: Demonstrate advanced APT campaign analysis including malware reverse engineering with detailed technical documentation.

**Implementation Requirements**:
- Complex attack patterns analyzed with demonstrated zero-day research methodology
- Comprehensive supply chain vulnerability assessment with clear documentation
- Analysis includes detailed methodology documentation, technical findings, indicators of compromise, and mitigation recommendations
- Evidence includes reverse engineering outputs, attack pattern analysis, and vulnerability assessment results

## Assessment Criteria

Each project component is evaluated based on:

1. **Technical Implementation** (40%)
   - Functionality and effectiveness of deployed solutions
   - Quality of technical implementation and configuration
   - Evidence of proper testing and validation

2. **Documentation Quality** (30%)
   - Comprehensive technical documentation
   - Clear methodology and process documentation
   - Professional reporting standards

3. **Analysis & Research** (20%)
   - Depth of threat analysis and research
   - Quality of findings and recommendations
   - Evidence of advanced analytical capabilities

4. **Integration & Operations** (10%)
   - Successful platform integration
   - Operational metrics and validation
   - Evidence of sustained operations
