# STAR Example: Automating Image Watermarking

**Question:** Tell me about a time when you improved a workflow or solved a complex problem at work.

## Situation
At my current job, the team needed to extract all clean images (without watermark) from a project where each piece had two folders: one with clean images and one with watermarked images. They wanted to add a new watermark and upload them to another website.

## Task
My responsibility was to copy all clean images and apply the new watermark while keeping the folder structure intact. Doing this manually would have taken at least two weeks.

## Action
I wrote a Python script that automatically went through every clean folder, applied the new watermark to each image, and saved them in a new directory while maintaining the same folder structure.

## Result
The script processed around 15,000 images in about 3 hours, saving the team at least two weeks of manual work. This solution significantly improved efficiency and allowed the team to meet the project deadline easily.
